How is the world warming?

The SSPs are “what-if” storylines the climate research community (IIASA-led) built around 2010 so CMIP6 models could be compared on common assumptions. They are not forecasts. Each pairs a scenario with an emissions (and thus climate) trajectory.

Scenarios

SSP1-2.6
A greener world. Emissions fall quickly as the world shifts to clean energy, uses land more carefully, and population grows more slowly. Warming levels off early, closer to the goals set in the Paris Agreement.
SSP2-4.5
A middle path. Some progress is made, but slowly and unevenly, and emissions keep rising for a while before peaking. Roughly what happens if today’s trends continue with modest improvements.
SSP5-8.5
A high-emissions world. Coal and oil keep powering fast economic growth, energy use climbs, and there is little effort to cut emissions. The classic worst-case picture of what could happen.
